Important Milestones

Plateau State was created in 1976. Mangu is one of the Local Government Areas that make up the state today.

The Plateau State government reports that the Potato Value Chain Support Project began in 2017. The initiative focused on improving potato production and value addition.

Plateau State reported renewed implementation efforts for the potato value-chain project, helping move the initiative toward completion.

In July 2026, Plateau State reported the commissioning of a Potato Value Chain Project, a tissue-culture laboratory, and modern agricultural equipment in Mangu Local Government Area.

Agriculture and Mangu

Agriculture is an important part of life and economic activity in Plateau State. Government investment information identifies Mangu as an area associated with agricultural production.

Recent investment in potato production and processing also shows the continuing importance of agriculture to the area.
